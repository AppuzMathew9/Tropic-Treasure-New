Company Profile 2026 · Quality Framework

Confidence Built on Evidence.

Our approach combines responsible sourcing, product verification, documentation and ongoing supplier development to support product integrity and dependable trade relationships.

Quality & Trade Readiness

Five Pillars of Verification

Every product enquiry is anchored in structured, auditable evidence from supplier evaluation through final shipment documentation.

Pillar 01

Supplier Evaluation

We review supplier business credentials, food safety information, supply capability and supporting documents.

We establish long-term relationships with credible processors and growers at origin, checking manufacturing practices, facility standards, processing hygiene, and historical compliance.

Pillar 02

Product Verification

We review specifications, samples and certificates of analysis (COAs), coordinating independent testing where required by the product, buyer or destination market.

Product verification bridges the expectation gap between source and end-buyer. Pre-shipment samples are drawn and verified against agreed technical specifications prior to container loading.

Parameter Check

Physical & Grading

Size, density, color, foreign matter, moisture

Chemical Testing

Essential Oils & Actives

Piperine, curcumin, volatile oil content

Safety Analysis

Micro & Contaminants

Aflatoxin, pesticide residues, heavy metals

Pillar 03

Traceability & Documentation

We review available origin, supplier and batch records alongside specifications and, where applicable, allergen, nutritional and packaging information.

  • Product Technical Specifications
  • Certificates of Analysis (COAs)
  • Allergen & Non-GMO Declarations
  • Nutritional Profile Information
  • Batch Traceability & Origin Records
  • Packaging Specifications & Pallet Maps
Pillar 04

Trade Readiness

We review packaging requirements and coordinate commercial and shipment documentation against agreed supply arrangements.

International trade succeeds on precision. We align bill of lading, commercial invoices, packing lists, phytosanitary certificates, certificate of origin, and destination customs data ahead of arrival.

Pillar 05

Supporting Credentials

Company registration records are available for due diligence. Relevant supplier and product certifications are reviewed for validity, scope and applicability to the proposed supply.

Both entities (Tropic Treasure Limited in the UK and Tropic Treasure Exim (OPC) Private Limited in India) operate in strict compliance with corporate and statutory trade regulations.
