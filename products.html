01 · Origin Spices

Spices

Black Peppercorns · Green Cardamom Pods · Ginger · Turmeric · Chilli · Cloves · Nutmeg

Sourced directly from historical spice regions in Kerala and across southern India. Each crop is verified for aroma, density, moisture levels and volatile oil content.

Black Peppercorns: Tellicherry Extra Bold (TGSEB), Special Bold, Malabar Garbled.
Green Cardamom Pods: Alleppey Green Extra Bold (AGEB), Bold 7mm–8mm, Split & Seeds.
Ginger: Whole Dried Unbleached Cochin Ginger, Flakes, Fine Ground Powder.
Turmeric: Alleppey Finger Turmeric (high curcumin content), Polished, Ground.
Chilli: Teja, S17, Byadgi, Stemless, Crushed flakes & Ground.
Cloves & Nutmeg: Handpicked whole cloves, Whole nutmeg with mace flower.

02 · Origin Coffee

Coffee

Indian Arabica · Robusta · Roasted Coffee

Grown in the high-altitude, biodiverse shade canopy of the Western Ghats. Known internationally for low acidity, full body, and distinct spice undertones.

Indian Arabica: Plantation A, Plantation PB, Monsooned Malabar AA.
Robusta: Parchment AB, Cherry AB, Cherry PB with high body and crema yield.
Roasted Coffee: Whole roasted beans, buyer-specific roast profiles, foodservice ground.

03 · Origin Nuts

Cashew Kernels

Cashew Kernels

India is the historic center of premium cashew processing. We supply export-grade kernels carefully graded by kernel count, color and uniformity.

W-180 (King of Cashews): Largest size kernel, premium confectionery & retail.
W-210 & W-240 (Jumbo): Generous whole kernels for roasting, gifting and trade.
W-320 (Standard Grade): The international trade benchmark whole white cashew.
Splits, Butts & Pieces (WS, LP, SP): Food manufacturing, confectionery & bakery.

04 · Spice Extracts

Oleoresins

Turmeric · Clove · Ginger · Black Pepper

Concentrated natural extracts retaining the characteristic aroma, flavor profile and active constituents of the raw spice. Ideal for food manufacturing, seasoning blends, beverages and nutraceuticals.

Turmeric Oleoresin: Standardized curcuminoids, natural yellow coloring and aroma.
Clove Oleoresin: Deep warm aroma with standardized eugenol content.
Ginger Oleoresin: Pungent gingerol and shogaol profile with aromatic volatile fractions.
Black Pepper Oleoresin: Standardized piperine content and robust aromatic pepper oil.
