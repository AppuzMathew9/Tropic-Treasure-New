Founder & Group Managing Director

Tropic Treasure Limited (UK) & Tropic Treasure Exim Pvt Ltd (India)

“
“Every ingredient begins at origin—with a place, a producer and a journey.”

Growing up in Kerala taught me to value India’s rich agricultural heritage and the dedication of the farming communities who nurture it. That appreciation inspired the founding of Tropic Treasure and my commitment to connect their produce directly with international food enterprises.

International trade should not be opaque. By operating directly through our own entities in both Kerala and the United Kingdom, we remove unnecessary layers, guarantee verified traceability, and ensure buyers receive precisely what was contracted.

Our ambition is simple: to build a business customers can rely on and suppliers can be proud to partner with. I look forward to working together.
